## I. How writers should cite

1. Prefer **claim card** language over paraphrasing freehand.  
2. Always attach: **date, method/N, geography, tier, caveat**.  
3. Never stack three tier-5 surveys to “prove” ROI.  
4. If only tier-5 available, label as **hypothesis / framing**.  
5. Link to **original URL** in the ledger—not secondary media.

These numbers are hypothetical. They are not promised results or industry benchmarks. No row is a prediction. Real closings come in whole numbers and take months to arrive. The model also assumes your lead quality, agent capacity, and mix of deals stay the same. And it assumes you can tell which closings the change produced.

The more leads you have, the more a small improvement is worth. No reliable study shows how many closings these changes add for a brokerage. So use this to set the bar a trial has to clear. Then measure the trial against your own starting numbers.

Reading your results

What the score does and does not mean.

The score shows which workflow is weakest. It does not grade your brokerage against others. The research behind the playbook found no reliable published benchmark to compare against. The "fix this first" pick is a rule of thumb. It is your weakest workflow, weighted toward the ones your business depends on most.

Safeguards score lowest

Fix these first, whatever else is weak. Automation repeats what you already do on a bigger scale, gaps included. That goes for consent records, fair housing checks, and human review.

Your main workflow scores lowest

The workflow your business depends on most is running on habit. The "fix this first" pick weighs this most heavily. It is usually the clearest place to start.

Have the broker-owner, a team leader, and the transaction coordinator each take it on their own. Where their answers differ is usually where to look hardest.

The 45-day path

From diagnostic to a first trial.

1.Days 1 to 10. Diagnose. Map how a lead becomes a closing. Review your CRM setup, lead sources, access rights, and handoffs. Measure where you are today.
2.Days 11 to 20. Redesign. Decide how the workflow should run: the rules, who owns each step, what happens when something goes wrong, and where a person signs off. Then pick the simplest setup that does all of that.
3.Days 21 to 35. Build. Connect the tools and set up the automation. Test what happens with duplicates, opt-outs, and problems that need a manager. Train the people who will use it.
4.Days 36 to 45. Check. Compare the results with your starting numbers, and decide on the evidence whether to expand. Keep watching closings, which take longer than 45 days to show up.

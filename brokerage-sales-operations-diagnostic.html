Reading your result

What the score does and does not mean.

The score is a structured way to see which workflow is weakest, not a measurement of your brokerage. It has no benchmark behind it; the research behind the playbook found no credible published benchmark for brokerage sales operations at this level. The recommendation is a heuristic: the weakest workflow, weighted toward the ones your operating model leans on.

The 45-day path

From diagnostic to a controlled pilot.

1.Days 1 to 10. Diagnose. Map the lead-to-close process, review CRM configuration, lead sources, permissions, and handoffs, and establish baseline metrics.
2.Days 11 to 20. Redesign. Define the future-state workflow: business rules, ownership, exception handling, approval checkpoints, and the simplest architecture that meets them.
3.Days 21 to 35. Implement. Configure integrations and automation rules, test the failure cases, including duplicates, opt-outs, and escalations, and train the people who use it.
4.Days 36 to 45. Validate. Measure against the baseline, decide on evidence whether to expand, and keep watching closed transactions, which take longer than the pilot to show.

When this becomes urgent

The moments that put it on your desk.

Backlog runs to a year or more while key licensed roles stay open for months.
Principals are at capacity, with winning work, checking work, and client calls all competing for the same few people.
The firm's distinctive design intelligence is concentrated in a few heads near retirement, and succession is a hope rather than a plan.
Owners and public clients have started asking in RFQs how the firm uses AI, and the current answer is individual improvisation.

What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Research, first drafts, and assembly come off licensed desks, so senior hours concentrate on design judgment, checking, and clients.

Every pursuit and every project starts from the firm's best prior work, with provenance, instead of from a blank sheet.

The operating picture of the portfolio assembles itself from live project artifacts instead of consuming a layer of senior time.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Project-level boundaries: one client's confidential material never surfaces in another client's set.
QA/QC review and responsible charge unchanged: licensed professionals retain every design decision and the seal.
Provenance on every reused detail and specification, so the firm always knows what its documents are built from.
A disclosure answer written before owners ask, one a managing principal would happily read aloud.
Proof, not promises

When this becomes urgent

The moments that put it on your desk.

Every mandate crosses the research bench before anything else can happen, and the queue, not the client, sets time to longlist.
Market maps, pitch materials, and candidate profiles keep being rebuilt from scratch for territories the firm has worked for years.
Client updates consume consultant evenings and still slip, assembled from memory rather than from the record of the search.

Where the leverage is, for your chair.

1.Research-to-Longlist. Mapping and profile assembly move onto governed rails with provenance, so researchers verify and enrich instead of hunting from zero.
2.Search-to-Client Reporting. Updates synthesized from the live record of each search, edited and owned by the consultant, on a cadence that gets kept.

What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Assembly leaves the critical path: maps, profiles, and drafts run on rails instead of on researcher evenings.

Every client update draws from the live search record, so cadence stops depending on who had time on Friday.

Load and stall signals surface per desk from the record, instead of arriving anecdotally after the client noticed.

The controls that make it trustworthy
An approved source list for research, so every profile is built from sources the firm could defend to the client.
Access that mirrors the search team, provisioned and revoked with it.
A consultant edits and owns every update and every approach before it ships.
Disclosure rules by stage, so no candidate is named to a client before the conversation has earned it.
Retention and reuse rules applied at mandate close: keep the map, never the confidences.
Proof, not promises

What we would baseline and measure.

No borrowed benchmarks and no invented results. These are the numbers your own pilot would stand on:

Days from mandate signature to a client-ready longlist
Searches active per consultant without quality slippage
Client update cadence kept versus promised
Time-to-shortlist and time-to-placement by mandate type
What the published evidence says

In a preregistered experiment published in Science in 2023, access to a general AI assistant cut time on occupation-specific professional writing tasks by about 40 percent and raised judged quality by about 18 percent, across roughly 453 college-educated professionals.

Noy & Zhang, Science, 2023

Controlled, task-level writing results, evaluator-scored; not search deliverables, client mandates, or firm-level capacity. It locates the drafting layer as the opportunity; only your own baseline shows what it returns.

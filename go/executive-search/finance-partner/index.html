What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

The cost side consolidates: one program cost, counted once, with each workflow carrying only its marginal cost.

The value side gets honest: baselined workflows, measured deltas, and freed hours assigned to named destinations.

Every number in the case traces to a source, because in a firm that sells judgment the business case is a work sample.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Baselines captured per desk before any pilot, so every capacity claim has a before.
One owner and one source of record per metric, drawn from the live search record rather than recollection.
Adoption lag modeled explicitly, so value arriving in month eight was planned, not excused.
Freed consultant hours named to a destination before the case is approved: more searches, deeper coverage, or client work.
Ranges with stated assumptions in front of the partnership, never false points.
Proof, not promises

What we would baseline and measure.

No borrowed benchmarks and no invented results. These are the numbers your own pilot would stand on:

Program cost, total and per active workflow
Searches active per consultant, against baseline
Days to longlist and time-to-placement deltas on piloted workflows
Revenue per consultant, the honest growth number
What the published evidence says

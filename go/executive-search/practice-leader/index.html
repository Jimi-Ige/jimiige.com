What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Profiles and market context arrive assembled with sources attached, so consultants verify and judge instead of hunting.

Interview notes reach the file organized for correction, so the record improves without the read being delegated.

The longlist carries its reasons: why each name stayed or left, which is the audit trail of the lens.

The controls that make it trustworthy
A consultant's read on every longlist before the client sees it, with the reasons for every cut recorded.
The verdict human by explicit rule: no model makes a reject decision, and references remain conversations between people who trust each other.
Outreach capped at what a human relationship could plausibly carry, drafted in the consultant's register.
Approved sources only, so every profile could be defended to the client that paid for it.

What the published evidence says

The field experiment that measured large gains from AI on tasks inside its capability frontier also found professional quality fell when consultants relied on it for tasks outside that frontier.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

Task-level evidence from one elite consulting firm, not a search firm, and the frontier moves with the model and the domain. In search the clearest outside-frontier move is letting a model make reject decisions; the control is a human verdict on every cut, not prohibition.

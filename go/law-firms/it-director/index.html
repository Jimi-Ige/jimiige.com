What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

The sanctioned path becomes the fastest path, which is the only adoption strategy that has ever beaten shadow IT.

Access follows matter staffing and ethical screens automatically, so boundaries are enforced by the platform, not by policy documents.

Every AI-assisted artifact carries its source trail, so guideline responses and client audits get answered from the system instead of from archaeology.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Role-based access mirroring matter teams and ethical screens, provisioned and revoked with staffing.
An approved-tool list with a real intake path, so new needs have somewhere to go besides the shadows.
Provenance and retention rules as platform defaults, tuned to what each client's guidelines require.
An incident path that assumes a miss will eventually happen and names who does what when it does.

What the published evidence says

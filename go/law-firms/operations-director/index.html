What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Assembly leaves the critical path: retrieval, drafting, and synthesis run on rails instead of on people's evenings.

Matter openings become a tracked flow with owners and clear gates, instead of a relay of emails.

Budget signals arrive while the phase is open, so managing a matter stops meaning reconstructing it afterward.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Access that mirrors matter staffing and ethical screens, provisioned and revoked with the team.
Conflicts clearance ahead of everything: no pitch ships and no matter opens on an incomplete check.
Verification and quality criteria written into the review points where reviewers actually work.
Named sign-out on everything that leaves the firm, so ship decisions have owners.
Retention and reuse rules applied at matter close, in line with each client's guidelines.

What the published evidence says

In a preregistered experiment published in Science, access to a general AI assistant cut time on occupation-specific professional writing tasks by about 40 percent while raising judged quality by about 18 percent (N of about 453).

Noy & Zhang, Science, 2023

Task-level results on general professional writing, not legal drafting under privilege and not full matters. Quality was evaluator-scored on experimental outputs. Do not treat as firm-level realization or margin improvement; treat it as the reason first-draft turnaround is worth baselining.

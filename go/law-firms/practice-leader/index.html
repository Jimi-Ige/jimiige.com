What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

First drafts are grounded in the group's own vetted material, so editing replaces assembling.

Reviewers see what was reused and where it came from, which makes review faster and sharper at once.

Verification of every authority becomes a written step with a name on it, instead of an assumption about who checked.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Attorney review before anything leaves the firm, exactly as today: what changes is the quality of what arrives at the gate.
A curated precedent bank with owners and review dates, so drafts build from the group's best, not the document store's worst.
Client and matter walls mirroring the firm's ethical screens, so confidences never cross-pollinate.
A written answer on AI use you can give any client without checking who is asking.

What the published evidence says

The field experiment that reported AI gains on tasks inside its capability frontier also reported professional quality falling when AI was relied on for tasks outside that frontier.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

Consulting tasks at one elite firm, not legal work, and where the frontier sits shifts with the model and the domain. In a law practice the outside-frontier failure looks like a fabricated citation or a conclusion never checked against authority, which is why verification stays a written step with a name on it.

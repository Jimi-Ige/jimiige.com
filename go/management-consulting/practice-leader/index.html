What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

First drafts are grounded in the practice's own vetted material, so editing replaces assembling.

Reviewers see what was reused and where it came from, which makes review faster and sharper at once.

Lessons learned get filed as part of shipping, so the practice's edge compounds instead of evaporating.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Human review before anything client-facing, exactly as today: what changes is the quality of what arrives at the gate.
Curated sources with owners and review dates, so drafts are built from the practice's best, not the file share's worst.
Engagement walls, so client confidences never cross-pollinate.
A written answer on AI use you can give any client without checking who is asking.

What the published evidence says

The same field experiment that reported in-frontier gains also reported consultant quality falling when AI was applied to tasks outside its capability frontier.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

Which tasks sit outside the frontier shifts with the model and the domain. Accountable human review remains a standing professional requirement, independent of average productivity gains.

What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

The cost side consolidates: one program cost, counted once, with each workflow carrying only its marginal cost.

The value side gets honest: baselined workflows, measured deltas, and freed senior hours assigned to a named destination.

Freed senior hours translate through the leverage ratio, so the case is written in margin rather than in hours nobody redeployed.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Baselines captured before any pilot, so every claim has a before.
One owner and one source of record per metric.
Adoption lag modeled explicitly, so value arriving in month eight was planned, not excused.
Ranges with stated assumptions in front of the partnership, never false points.

What the published evidence says

The hour-savings figures that circulate hardest in this market are respondent predictions from vendor-sponsored surveys, not savings measured against a baseline. No credible study of AI's return for a mid-market professional-services firm exists in the reviewed public record.

Evidence gaps G-01 and G-02, research/evidence-gaps.md

Predicted hours may never convert to utilization, realization, or margin; only a baseline and honest measurement can show whether they do. The gap is stated rather than filled, because a number invented to close it would not survive the first question.

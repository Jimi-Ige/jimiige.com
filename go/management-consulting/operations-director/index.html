What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Assembly work leaves the critical path: retrieval, drafting, and synthesis run on rails instead of on people.

Handoffs become records instead of conversations, so nothing promised gets lost between sale and delivery.

Capacity signals come from staffing and timesheet reality, visible while there is still time to act on them.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Role-based access that mirrors staffing, so the tools reach exactly what each team should reach.
Quality criteria written into the review points where reviewers actually work.
Named approval points, so ship decisions have owners instead of committees.
Retention and reuse rules set at engagement close, while the boundaries are still fresh.

What the published evidence says

In a field experiment at one elite consulting firm, consultants using AI on tasks inside its capability frontier were reported to complete more work, faster, and at higher judged quality than the control group.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

Task-level results at one elite firm; not proof for mid-market firms or firm-level margin. The same study reported quality falling when AI was used outside the frontier, which is what the review gates are for.

When this becomes urgent

The moments that put it on your desk.

Senior creatives are producing versions instead of directing work, and the review calendar has become the studio's bottleneck.
The work has started to sound like the category's default answer, and nobody can point to where the drift began.
Juniors spend their years resizing and adapting instead of learning the judgment that makes them promotable.
A client asked how AI was used on their campaign, and the account team's answer did not match the studio's.

What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Exploration widens, because surveying ten territories no longer costs what surveying three used to, and the team rejects from abundance rather than settling at 2am.

The mechanical spread, sizes, formats, versions, and first-pass adaptations, comes off creative desks and arrives at review instead of at midnight.

What was reused and where it came from travels with the work, which makes creative review faster and sharper at the same time.

The controls that make it trustworthy
The support boundary written down: research, references, and variants are assisted; the idea and the call are not.
Internal creative review before anything a client sees, including the brand-guideline check and the AI-assistance check.
Governed reference and asset libraries with rights status visible before anything is pinned to a board.
Human creative direction documented on every deliverable, kept with the job file rather than in someone's memory.
Junior work redesigned rather than deleted, so the people who will hold the bar in five years still learn how.

What the published evidence says

The field experiment that measured large gains from AI inside its capability frontier also found professional quality fell when people relied on it for tasks outside that frontier, across 758 consultants at one elite consulting firm.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

Consulting tasks at one elite firm, so the transfer to creative work is an inference, and the frontier itself moves with the model and the domain. Nobody has credibly measured what AI does to the quality of an idea. That gap is exactly why the call stays human and the review gate stays where it is.

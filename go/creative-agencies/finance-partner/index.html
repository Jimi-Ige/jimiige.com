What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

The cost side consolidates: one program cost, counted once, with each workflow carrying only its marginal cost.

The value side gets honest: baselined workflows, measured deltas, and freed studio hours assigned to a named destination before the program starts.

Every number in the case traces to a source, because the case is a work sample of how this agency reports on itself.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Baselines captured before any pilot, so every claim has a before.
One owner and one source of record per measure, agreed before the first number is presented.
Adoption lag modeled explicitly, so value arriving in month eight was planned rather than excused.
Ranges with stated assumptions in front of leadership, never false points.

What the published evidence says

The strongest causal evidence available here is task-level, not margin-level: in a preregistered experiment published in Science, access to a general AI assistant cut time on occupation-specific professional writing tasks by about 40 percent and raised evaluator-judged quality by about 18 percent, across roughly 453 college-educated professionals.

Noy & Zhang, Science, 2023

Controlled writing tasks, evaluator-scored, not client engagements and not creative concepting, and explicitly not a firm-level margin or realization result. No public source for creative-agency utilization or realization economics passed this library's evidence bar either, so every number in your case has to come from your own baseline.

What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Research, first drafts, and retrieval come off senior desks, so architect hours concentrate on scope, price, and the architecture call.

Every pursuit and every build starts from the firm's own reviewed patterns, with provenance, instead of from a blank page.

The operating picture of the portfolio assembles itself from live delivery artifacts instead of consuming a layer of delivery management.

Provenance on every reused and generated component, so the firm always knows what its promises are built from.
A written answer on AI use inside client environments, ready before a client's security team asks for it.

What the published evidence says

In a field experiment at one elite global firm, consultants using AI on tasks inside its capability frontier were reported to complete more work, faster, and at higher judged quality than the control group.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

Task-level results at one elite global firm on consulting-style knowledge tasks, the closest analog to this work and still not engineering delivery or mid-market proof. The same study reported quality falling when AI was used outside the frontier, and where that frontier sits shifts with the model and the task, which is what the review gates are for. No public causal study measures AI's return for a mid-market IT consulting firm.

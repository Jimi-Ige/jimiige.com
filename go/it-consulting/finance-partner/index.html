You have seen the vendor math: day-one payback, hours saved that nobody redeployed, benefits counted three times. The governed version is different by construction: baselines before pilots, program cost counted once, adoption lag priced in, ranges instead of points, and freed capacity named to a destination so it lands in margin instead of on the bench. What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

The cost side consolidates: one program cost, counted once, with each workflow carrying only its marginal cost.

The value side gets honest: baselined workflows, measured deltas, and freed hours assigned to a named destination.

Every number in the case traces to a source, because in this firm a business case is a work sample of your own estimation discipline.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Baselines captured before any pilot, so every claim has a before.
One owner and one source of record per metric.
Adoption lag modeled explicitly, so value arriving in month eight was planned, not excused.
Ranges with stated assumptions in front of leadership, never false points.

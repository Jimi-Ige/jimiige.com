What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Tickets arrive enriched with client, environment, and the history of this exact issue, so routing takes seconds instead of archaeology.

Tier one works from the firm's own documented fixes for this environment, verified against the live system before anything is touched.

Documentation stops being a virtue reserved for quiet weeks and becomes part of the definition of a closed ticket.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Retrieval walled by client, mirroring the tenant isolation the rest of the stack already enforces.
A curated runbook corpus with owners and review dates, not the whole documentation share pointed at a model.
A published escalation ladder, with the evidence each step must carry named in advance.
Change approval by an accountable engineer on every consequential action, including the ones automation proposes.

What the published evidence says

In a staggered deployment of a generative AI assistant across 5,179 customer-support agents, issues resolved per hour rose about 14 percent on average and about 34 percent for novice and lower-skilled agents, with minimal gains for the most experienced.

Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, NBER w31161, 2023

The setting is single-tenant software support chat, many agents outside the U.S., and agents could ignore suggestions. It is not multi-tenant MSP work with SLAs and privileged access, so on your desk it is a transfer hypothesis to test against a baseline, not a result to bank.

Walk into the QBR with instruments, not a defense.

The QBR is the one hour in the quarter when the client actually sees the service they have been paying for, and where the contract quietly renews or quietly begins to die. Governed AI rebuilds the pack from live service records instead of from the night before: ticket and SLA synthesis, incident narratives with root cause attached, and roadmap recommendations tied to evidence from that client's own environment. Your read still decides what the meeting is about.

When this becomes urgent

The moments that put it on your desk.

QBR packs get assembled the night before from ticketing exports and memory, and a renewal recently wobbled.
The pack defends last quarter instead of setting up the client's next year.
Recurring issues are visible to the client before they are visible in your reporting.
Roadmap recommendations read as selling, because nothing in the pack traces to the client's own environment.
The workflows that move it

Where the leverage is, for your chair.

1.Service-to-QBR Insight. Every figure in the pack drillable to tickets, alerts, or incidents in one step, and drafted for your review before it ships.
2.Quote-to-Contract. Expansion scoped from the maintained catalog, with exclusions stated in the agreement instead of discovered at the first ticket.
3.Ticket-to-Knowledge. The resolution record your credibility rests on, complete enough to show the client what changed and why it will not recur.

The full treatment is in the playbook: Scale Service Delivery with Governed AI.

What AI changes

The work moves. The accountability does not.

Service synthesis, incident narrative, and trend detection are drafted from live records instead of reconstructed from memory.

Recurring tickets, aging assets, and looming exposures surface per client before the client discovers them first.

Preparation time moves from assembling the pack to deciding what the meeting should be about.

The controls that make it trustworthy
Your read before any pack reaches a client. Synthesis prepares the story; it does not get to tell it.
Every number drillable to its source record, so a figure challenged in the room is answered in the room.
The quarter reported as the client lived it, including what went wrong and what changed because of it.
Client-walled context, so nothing from another environment ever appears in this client's pack.
Proof, not promises

What we would baseline and measure.

No borrowed benchmarks and no invented results. These are the numbers your own pilot would stand on:

Recurring-ticket rate per client, trending down as root causes close
Mean time to resolution by ticket class, as the client experiences it
Preparation hours per QBR, and where the returned hours go
Effort versus agreement value by client, visible before the renewal conversation
What the published evidence says

The field experiment behind the jagged-frontier finding showed professional performance degrading when AI was relied on for tasks outside its capability frontier, even as in-frontier work improved sharply.

Dell'Acqua et al., 2023 (SSRN)

One elite global firm, consultants on experimental business tasks, not engineers in client environments, and where the frontier sits shifts with the model and the domain. For a client-facing pack the implication is a human read before anything ships, because a polished summary that contradicts the quarter the client lived costs more than a rough one that matches it.

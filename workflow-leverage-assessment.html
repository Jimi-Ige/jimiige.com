The engagement · Professional services

The Workflow Leverage & Trust Assessment

A fixed-scope working session for professional-services firms. We map two or three of your workflows, baseline them honestly, name the constraint that is actually holding growth, and design the governed pilot that tests what AI-enabled leverage would do about it. Evidence first, program second.

This is the operations engagement behind Governed AI Operations for Professional Services. If your question is compliance posture against a specific regime, the Expert Readiness Assessment is the instrument for that.

Who this is for

Firms that sell judgment, and want more capacity without diluting it.

Consulting firms, CPA firms, law firms, architecture and engineering practices, search firms, agencies, and managed service providers share one operating truth: growth is constrained by senior attention, and trust is the license to operate. The assessment fits when one of these is on your desk:

The growth plan assumes hiring the market will not supply at a workable price
Senior people are at capacity while the pipeline is still growing
The firm keeps recreating work it has already produced and sold
Clients have started asking how the firm uses AI on their work
Leadership wants an AI answer and the current one is improvisation
AI spend is accumulating with no measured operating return
The engagement

What you leave with.

You leave with
A workflow map and baseline for two or three chosen workflows: where the hours go, what gets recreated, where the handoffs leak.
The constraint, named: whether growth is bound by winning work, shaping it, delivering it, or reviewing it, with the evidence that says so.
A leverage map: the moves with the best leverage-to-risk trade, and the ones to decline, stated plainly.
The control set, designed in: engagement boundaries, approved sources, review and approval points, traceability, and retention, sized to your obligations and your clients' expectations.
A 90-day pilot plan with owners, quality criteria, KPIs against your baseline, and an honest range for the business case. No vendor math.

How it works

1.Read your industry's playbook in the library. Free, no email wall.
2.A first conversation to confirm fit and pick the workflows worth examining.
3.The fixed-scope working sessions: mapping, baselining, and control design with the people who own the work.
4.The readout: baseline, constraint, leverage map, control set, and the 90-day pilot plan.
5.Optional: the bounded pilot itself, two workflows for ninety days, evidence before scale.

Professional judgment stays with your professionals throughout. For licensed practices, nothing in this engagement touches the judgments and sign-offs the license exists for.

The method is evidence-anchored: the published task-level research on AI productivity and its limits, and the professional-duty guidance for licensed practices, shape what gets scored. Firm-level claims come only from your own baseline, because the honest literature does not yet contain a mid-market payback number to borrow.

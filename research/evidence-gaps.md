# Evidence gaps

Gaps are stated, never filled. Each entry below is a place where the published
content deliberately declines to make a claim, because no source meeting the
standard in `evidence-policy.md` exists. Where the content touches the subject at
all, it says the gap out loud.

Never delete a gap entry. When a gap closes, move it to the closed section with
the claim ID that closed it.

## Open gaps

### G-01. Causal ROI for mid-market professional-services firms

**What is missing:** any causal study of governed AI programmes in US
professional-services firms of roughly 20 to 200 employees, the actual target
segment.

**Why it matters:** it is the single number a firm's leadership most wants, and
the one most often fabricated in this market.

**What the content does instead:** states that no credible published study exists
and that the baseline must come from the firm's own pursuit or delivery cycle.
Appears in the Management Consulting playbook close, the outcome page's
measurement section, and the Business Play Card.

**Do not:** invent a payback period, quote a vendor case study as though it were
research, or generalize the elite-firm field experiment to mid-market economics.

### G-02. Realization-rate impact of AI-assisted drafting

**What is missing:** evidence on what AI drafting does to realization, write-downs,
scope, and pricing in a professional-services firm.

**Why it matters:** the margin thesis depends on saved hours converting to value
rather than being absorbed into unbilled work or discounted.

**What the content does instead:** treats "hours saved equals margin" as an
explicit fallacy and requires the firm to name where freed capacity goes.

**Do not:** assume time saved is profit.

### G-03. Quality outcomes under real client-confidentiality constraints

**What is missing:** studies of AI-assisted professional work performed inside
genuine confidentiality boundaries, rather than on open-web tools with synthetic
tasks.

**Why it matters:** the governed configuration is the one being sold, and it is
not the configuration that was studied.

**What the content does instead:** cites task-level experiments for the mechanism
only, with the setting stated, and never presents them as client-ready evidence.

### G-04. Creative agency utilization and realization economics

**What is missing:** any primary source meeting the quality bar. The imported
research explicitly rejected one candidate claim here.

**What the content does instead:** the creative agencies playbook teaches the
economics qualitatively and states the gap in four places. No agency-economics
figure appears anywhere in the library.

### G-05. Executive search firm economics and AI outcomes

**What is missing:** primary economics for search firms, and any AI outcome study
in the sector. The available association material lacked extractable methods.

**What the content does instead:** the executive search playbook states the gap in
its overview evidence coda and sources block, and leans on professional-duty
sources instead.

### G-06. MSP margin, utilization, and seat economics

**What is missing:** a primary series with publicly extractable methods.

**What the content does instead:** the MSP playbook teaches seat economics
qualitatively, states the gap, and uses the customer-support productivity study
only as a labelled transfer hypothesis, never as direct MSP evidence.

### G-07. Professional liability and E&O claims data tied to generative AI

**What is missing:** insurer or claims data connecting generative AI use to
professional liability frequency or severity.

**Why it matters:** the Risk Guardian persona would act on it, and its absence is
why the risk argument is built on professional-duty obligations instead.

**Do not:** assert claims frequency, insurer posture, or premium impact.

### G-08. Knowledge-management ROI specific to prior-work reuse

**What is missing:** evidence on reuse systems in professional services
specifically.

**What the content does instead:** argues reuse from the operating mechanism and
the cross-study novice-gain pattern, labelled as an inference.

**Do not:** use generic knowledge-management folklore statistics.

### G-09. Architecture-specific measured AI productivity

**What is missing:** measured outcomes for architecture practice. Association
guidance exists, but guidance is not outcome evidence.

**What the content does instead:** cites the toolkit's existence and purpose only,
and says explicitly that it is not proof of productivity or risk reduction.

### G-10. IT consulting utilization as a distinct vertical

**What is missing:** vertical-specific utilization. The available benchmark is a
multi-vertical blend without a public split.

**What the content does instead:** states that the vertical split is not public
rather than implying the blended figure describes IT consulting.
